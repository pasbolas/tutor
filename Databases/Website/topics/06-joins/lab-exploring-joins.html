03 Exploring the football database with JOINs

Consider the following scenario: you have just joined a sports analytics company. Your first task is to explore the football database (clubs, players, player_attributes, player_contracts). The company wants insights about clubs, players, contracts, and performance. Use JOINs to answer the following company requests.

Request 1) List the names of all players and of the clubs they are currently contracted to.

Request 2) Find all players who have the attributes overall and potential recorded but no contract with any club.

Request 3) List the top 10 highest-paid players, showing their name, wage, and the club they play for (Hint: if you add LIMIT 10 to the end of your code, only the first 10 rows will be shown).

Request 4) Show the player name, club name, and contract_valid_until for players whose contracts expire in 2025.
